# Accessibility

Joe should be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, larger text or high contrast.

## Where it runs

Joe's interface runs in your web browser (Edge, Chrome, Firefox, Safari) on Windows, macOS and Linux, and can talk and listen when the voice add-on is installed.

## Motion

Joe's face in Talk (F4) blinks and moves his mouth while he speaks. If your computer is set to reduce motion, or you pick "Still" in Setup > Voice & Talk > "Joe's face in Talk", he still changes face for each state (listening, thinking, talking, happy, worried, sleepy) but nothing blinks or flaps. "Off" hides him. The face is decorative and hidden from screen readers; everything he says is in the chat as text.

## Known limitations

- The retro style uses bracketed text buttons and a terminal look. All of it is real text, but contrast in some themes may be low.
- Some long jobs report progress only in the activity log, not as screen-reader announcements.

## Report a barrier

If something is hard or impossible for you to use, please [open an issue](https://github.com/sirgijoe/joe-ai/issues/new/choose) and say what you were trying to do and what got in the way, plus your device and any assistive technology you use. Accessibility problems are treated as bugs.
