FOR JOSEPH: WHAT TO LOOK AT

  1. Search bar at the top: matches a tool's name, its one-line blurb, its example phrases and its category. Every word you type must match. Try “faces”, “pdf”, “voice”, “money”.
  2. ★ Favorites at the top, then Used lately (the last 5 tools you ran). Click ☆ on any tool. Favorites are kept in settings, so they survive updates and come along in a backup.
  3. Agents get their own section, with the five templates. The status strip under the tabs shows on every tab while one works.
  4. Categories open and close like Setup > Workflows. Joe remembers which ones you left open. Inside each one, tools are A–Z (in the language Joe's screens are in).
  5. Pictures is split (it had 20): fix, sort and describe (8) · change a picture (6) · make new ones (6).
