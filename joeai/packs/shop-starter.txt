PACK: Small shop starter
ABOUT: Product descriptions, a matching price list, an About page, an FAQ and product pictures.
ASK: What's your shop and what do you sell? (e.g. a candle shop selling soy candles)
=== JOE MISSION ===
RULES: This is for {for}. Warm, friendly, plain words. Never invent prices, reviews, awards or facts; write [CHECK] for anything unknown.
TASK: Product descriptions
CHUNKS: 4 x 5
FORMAT: Product name, then a short description of 30-50 words: what it is, what it's like, who it's for. Every name is different.
TASK: Price list
FROM: Product descriptions
CHUNKS: 4 x 5
HEADER: Name,Description,Price
SAVE: price-list.csv
FORMAT: One row per product. Description is one short line. Price is always [CHECK].
TASK: About page
PART: ## Our story. Who we are and why we started, in a warm, personal voice. Leave real names and dates as [CHECK].
PART: ## What we make. What we sell and what makes it special.
PART: ## Our promise. Quality, care and how we treat customers.
EXPORT: docx
TASK: FAQ
CHUNKS: 2 x 6
FORMAT: A question a customer would ask, then a short helpful answer. Shipping times, policies and prices are [CHECK].
TASK: Product pictures
TYPE: image
FROM: Product descriptions
CHUNKS: 2 x 4
STYLE: product
FORMAT: The product on a clean, softly lit surface, simple background, no text or logos.
=== END ===
