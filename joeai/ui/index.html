      ABOUT JOE
      
      
      

       MEET JOE
 ────────
 Meet 01000001 01001001. Humans can't say that, so we just call him Joe.
 JOE'S STORY
 ───────────
 01000001 01001001 (or Joe, to his immense relief) used to be a guy who
 tried to speed-run a software update by slamming a cup of coffee
 directly into his laptop's cooling fan. It instantly vaporized his
 physical form and fused his consciousness into a help desk assistant.
 Now he's trapped forever inside your PC, with one directive: make
 human lives easier. He spends eternity writing your product listings
 at 3 a.m., optimizing Wi-Fi routers for crying toddlers, and quietly
 questioning every life choice that led him to become a sentient .dll
 file named after a binary alphabet.

 WHY WE MADE IT
 ──────────────
 Good AI help shouldn't depend on how many tokens you can afford.
 Most of the work behind a finished piece is grunt work: first drafts,
 long lists, product listings, design notes, tables. A computer with a
 decent graphics card can do that overnight, for free, while you sleep.

 Joe hands that grunt work to a local model. In the morning you
 get the drafts plus a short report of what needs a human (or a bigger
 AI) to look at. You spend your time and money on the finishing touches,
 not the first ninety percent.

 Everything runs on your own PC. Your words, your business details and
 your ideas never leave it unless you choose to send them somewhere.

 WHY OPEN SOURCE
 ───────────────
 Software that runs on your machine and reads your files should be
 software you can read. Open source means anyone can check what it does,
 fix what's broken, and make it better. Nothing hidden, no account,
 no subscription, no tracking.

 It also means Joe can't be taken away or locked behind a
 paywall later. It belongs to everyone who uses it.

 ───────────────────
 Local models make mistakes. Joe marks doubts with [CHECK],
 [PRICE] and [CONFIRM], but always review before you publish, sell,
 or rely on anything it writes.

 PRIVACY

 are to Ollama and ComfyUI on your own computer, the display font
 from Google Fonts when you are online, the update check, and the
 phone ping if you turn it on (one short line, nothing from your files).
 Installs and model downloads (System check, F10) only happen after
 you say yes.

       GETTING STARTED
 1. Queue screen: type what you need in your own words,
    then MAKE A PLAN.
 2. Check Joe's plan. RUN IT (or CHANGE IT in the Builder).
 3. In the morning, Report (R) says what's done.
 Click the tabs along the top, or use the keys below.
 Hover over any button: the bottom line says what it does.
 Setup (F10) > SETUP HELPER walks through installing things.

 KEY   WHAT IT DOES
 ───   ─────────────────────────────────────────────
 F1    This help
 F2    Queue: describe what you need, run it, watch progress
       (ADVANCED shows the mission text)
 F3    Files: browse the workspace, preview, open
 F4    Builder: make a plan with simple forms, reorder or
       split, paste a whole mission, rerun past ones
 F6    Talk to Joe. Drop a file on the window (or ATTACH) and ask about it,
       or say "read it". Text, Word, PDF, web pages; pictures need a vision model.
       Dropped files are copied to Inbox\dropped.
       The mic is open while this screen is up: just talk,
       and Joe answers when you pause (MIC button mutes). Prefer
       push-to-talk? F10 > Voice, pick any key. You can always type.
       (Voice needs a one-time setup: F10 > System check.)
 F5    Run the mission  /  stop a running mission
       ADD TO QUEUE adds more work while Joe is busy; queued
       missions run one after another until all are done.
       If a run didn't finish, Joe offers to resume it on start.
 F8    Statistics
 F9    About Joe and the license
 F10   Setup
 Esc   Back to the queue (or leave the text box)
 R     Morning report (on the queue screen)
       PICTURES (under the queue) shows each picture or video as
       soon as it's made: BIG WINDOW, OPEN IN PLAYER, DETAILS F3

 FILES SCREEN
 ↑ ↓   Move        Enter  Open folder / open file
 Bksp  Up a folder O      Show in Explorer
 E     Edit (Notepad)     C  Copy full path
 L     Load a mission file into the mission box
 T     Thumbnails on/off (pictures and videos)
 K X S Review: keep / reject / star (press again to clear)
 M     Make an MP4 from a GIF, WebP or video (FFmpeg)
 P     Make Word + PDF copies of a .md file (Pandoc)

 Closing this window does NOT stop a run. Reopen Joe
 any time to check on it. Everything stays inside the workspace.
